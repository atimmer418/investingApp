Manage Your Beneficiaries

Add and manage the people who will inherit your investment account assets. Each beneficiary can be assigned a percentage of your total account value.

 Important Information

Understanding your Transfer on Death (TOD) beneficiary designations:

  • Primary beneficiaries must total 100% allocation
  • Contingent beneficiaries only receive assets if no primary beneficiaries can claim them
  • Processing time: Changes may take 1-2 business days to process with your broker
  • Keep updated: Review beneficiary information after major life events
This ensures your investments transfer quickly without going through probate.
