Test if your money will last through retirement using real market scenarios

How This Simulator Works

Monte Carlo simulation tests your retirement plan by running 1,000 different market scenarios. Here's what happens:

  • Random Market Conditions: Each scenario uses different stock/bond returns based on historical market volatility
  • Inflation Protection: Your withdrawal amount increases by 2.5% annually to maintain purchasing power
  • Balanced Portfolio: Assumes 70% stocks (10% average return) and 30% bonds (4% average return)
  • Success Rate: Shows the percentage of scenarios where your money lasts the full retirement period

Confidence Guide

90%+ Success Rate: Very confident - your plan should work in most market conditions

75-89% Success Rate: Moderately confident - consider small adjustments

Below 75%: High risk - significant plan changes recommended
