What is Monte Carlo?

Monte Carlo simulation tests your retirement plan by running 1,000 different market scenarios to see if your money lasts.

  • Each test uses different returns based on historical data
  • Your withdrawal increases 2.5% annually for inflation
  • Success rate = % of scenarios where money lasts full retirement
