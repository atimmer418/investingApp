Our Philosophy

Why This Is Our Default Portfolio

The Long-Term Growth Portfolio

We provide this default portfolio because guessing the next "big boom" stock is a headache that fights for marginal gains. This strategy is designed to pursue strong long-term growth while remaining broadly diversified without the complexity.

75% VTI – U.S. Total Stock Market
20% VXUS – International Stocks
5% VBR – Small-Cap Value Tilt
Why This Works
  • Max Growth: ~95% stocks with a history of outperforming over decades.
  • Diversification: Reduces risk by owning more of the market instead of individual stocks.
  • Global Exposure: 20% international avoids US-only risk without dragging returns.
  • Factor Tilt: 5% VBR captures the academically recognized "small-cap value" premium.
  • Simplicity: Reduces behavioral errors (no tinkering or timing).

This portfolio is provided as a default starting allocation. Users may customize their investments at any time. FRED does not provide personalized investment advice. Historically, this allocation has produced a nominal average annual return of ~11.5%. Future returns will vary. FRED projections use a conservative assumption of 10%.
