Why we recommend this

The "10/10" Growth Strategy

We provide this default portfolio because guessing the next "big boom" stock is a headache that fights for marginal gains. This strategy is designed for maximum long-term growth probability without the complexity.

75% VTI – U.S. Total Stock Market
20% VXUS – International Stocks
5% VBR – Small-Cap Value Tilt

Why This Works

  • Max Growth: ~95% stocks with a history of outperforming over decades.
  • Global Sweet Spot: 20% international avoids US-only risk without dragging returns.
  • Factor Tilt: 5% VBR captures the academically recognized "small-cap value" premium.
  • Simplicity: Reduces behavioral errors (no tinkering or timing).

*While no growth is truly guaranteed, this mix has historically never lost value over a 20-30 year period.
