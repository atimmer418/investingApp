Market Assumptions

FRED tests your plan using three market scenarios, each simulated 1,000 times.

Scenario Stocks Bonds Inflation
Conservative 8% / 20% vol 3.5% / 5.5% 2.5%
Expected 10% / 18% vol 4% / 5% 2.5%
Aggressive 11.5% / 16% vol 4.5% / 4.5% 2.5%

Each of the 1,000 scenarios draws annual returns from normal distributions (Box-Muller) calibrated to S&P 500 and Bloomberg Aggregate bond data, 1926–2024. Your withdrawal rises 2.5%/yr with inflation. A 70/30 stock/bond portfolio is used for the Traditional strategy; other strategies use their own allocations.

info Simulations are for educational purposes only and do not constitute investment advice.
