FRED-109 — Reframe the onboarding question to "When do you want work to be optional?"
You skipped FRED-187 (the Plaid paycheck spike) — here's a fresh single story. Today the first onboarding survey asks how much you can invest and tells you how long it'll take. FRED-109 flips that to lead with the question FRED is really about: "When do you want work to be optional?" — make the freedom timeline the thing you set, and let FRED back-solve the monthly investment it takes to get there. I ground-truthed the screen this morning:
  • components/surveyinitial/surveyinitial.component currently has an "I Can Invest… $X/mo" slider (input) + an "I Want To Retire With… $X/mo" slider, and computes "Estimated Time to Goal" in years as the output.
  • The math is a clean FIRE formula (months = ln((target·rate/PMT)+1)/ln(1+rate), 10% return, 4% withdrawal) — and it inverts cleanly: given a target time, solve for the required monthly investment (PMT = target·rate / ((1+rate)^months − 1)).
So this is a contained, frontend-only reframe of one onboarding component — no backend, schema, or auth. Tier 2 — no acceptance criteria in the backlog yet, so I've drafted them below for you to approve or edit; the build won't start until they're confirmed.
