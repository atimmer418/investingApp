Market assumptions

Every simulation draws yearly returns from these distributions, calibrated to S&P 500 and Bloomberg bond data, 1926–2024.

ScenarioStocksBondsInflation
Conservative8% · 20% vol3.5% · 5.5%2.5%
Expected10% · 18% vol4% · 5%2.5%
Aggressive11.5% · 16% vol4.5% · 4.5%2.5%

How it works. Each run generates 1,000 independent market paths for a 70/30 stock-bond portfolio. Your withdrawal rises 2.5% each year with inflation. A strategy "holds" in a path if money never runs out over your full horizon — its success rate is the share of the 1,000 paths that held.

info Simulations are educational estimates based on historical data — not guarantees or investment advice. Future markets can behave unlike the past.
